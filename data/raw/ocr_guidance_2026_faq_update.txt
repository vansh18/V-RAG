Background
----------

By mid-2026, the Fourth and Fifth Circuits, in Nguyen v. Trent Institute of Technology
and Castellano v. Trent Institute of Technology, and the Ninth Circuit, in Delacroix v.
Westbrook College of Law, remain in unresolved disagreement over how Harmon's essay
clarification applies to guidance instructing admissions readers to consider race-
related adversity narratives. The Supreme Court has not granted certiorari in any of
these cases as of the date of this guidance.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

Should the Office adopt a single nationwide position on the Fourth/Fifth Circuit versus
Ninth Circuit disagreement over essay-reading guidance, pending Supreme Court
resolution?

Guidance
--------

This updated FAQ summarizes the current, unresolved state of the law: institutions
within the Fourth and Fifth Circuits are bound by Nguyen and Castellano, which forbid
guidance specifically instructing readers to credit race-related adversity narratives,
while institutions within the Ninth Circuit are bound by Delacroix, which permits such
guidance so long as no fixed point value is tied to the presence of racial content.

The Office declines to adopt a single nationwide position pending further judicial
clarification, and recommends that institutions consult counsel regarding the rule
applicable in their circuit.

The Office reiterates its 2024 recommendation that, regardless of which circuit's rule
applies, essay-reading guidance should focus readers' attention on the substantive
individual content of each essay, and cautions that guidance resembling the rubric
struck down in Nguyen -- awarding credit based on the presence of racial content rather
than substantive individualized assessment -- remains unlawful in every circuit that has
addressed the question.

The Office notes that the underlying disagreement now spans three circuits and involves
substantial practical consequences for institutions operating campuses or admissions
offices in more than one circuit, and states its expectation that Supreme Court review
may ultimately be necessary, while emphasizing that it takes no position on how such
review should be resolved.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Nguyen v. Trent Institute of Technology, 98 F.4th 214 (4th Cir. 2024) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Summarizes the fourth circuit's rule.
* Delacroix v. Westbrook College of Law, 101 F.4th 782 (9th Cir. 2024) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Summarizes the ninth circuit's contrary rule.
* Castellano v. Trent Institute of Technology, 110 F.4th 305 (5th Cir. 2026) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Summarizes the fifth circuit's alignment with the fourth circuit.
* Office for Civil Rights Frequently Asked Questions on Admissions Practices After Harmon, OCR FAQ 2024-01 -- this opinion affirms that authority. Updates, without superseding, the office's 2024 faq.
