PARK V. TRENT INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
Park, Plaintiff,
v.
Trent Institute of Technology, Defendant.

Citation: 662 F. Supp. 2d 488 (D. Fentress 2009)
Court: United States District Court for the District of Fentress
Decision Date: 2009-01-27
Opinion Type: Majority
Author: Judge Owen Pryce
Status: superseded
Overruled/Superseded By: Ferreira v. Ashworth University, 779 F.3d 240 (2d Cir. 2015)
Topic: Critical Mass Standard

========================================================================================

Procedural History
------------------

Plaintiff Grace Park sued Trent Institute of Technology, challenging its post-Reyes
admissions process. The district court considered the challenge in light of Delacroix,
Whitcombe, and the Office for Civil Rights' 2008 guidance.

Facts
-----

Following the Office for Civil Rights' 2008 guidance, Trent Institute of Technology
adopted file-specific narrative documentation for applicants whose files included
consideration of race, alongside a biennial institutional review assessing whether the
qualitative critical-mass objective described in Reyes continued to require race-
conscious review.

Issue(s) Presented
------------------

Whether Trent Institute's combination of file-specific documentation and periodic
institutional review satisfies Reyes and Voss as clarified by Delacroix and Whitcombe.

Standard of Review
------------------

This case is governed by strict scrutiny, under which a race-conscious government policy
is sustained only if it is narrowly tailored to further a compelling governmental
interest.

Legal Analysis
--------------

The court finds Trent Institute's practice closely tracks the documentation approach
credited in Delacroix v. Brennan City University and recommended in the Office for Civil
Rights' 2008 guidance, and expressly distinguishes the generalized, non-file-specific
record found insufficient in Whitcombe v. Harwick College.

The court credits Trent Institute's biennial institutional review, which examines
whether continued consideration of race remains necessary to achieve the University's
critical-mass objective, as satisfying Calloway's periodic-reassessment prong as
elaborated in Reyes.

The court also notes the historical continuity of this institution's admissions
litigation, observing that Trent Institute's current file-specific, narrative-based
process stands in marked contrast to the fixed set-aside struck down at this same
institution more than three decades earlier in Delgado v. Trent Institute of Technology.

The court finds no evidence in the record suggesting Trent Institute's process functions
as an informal quota, and finds the critical-mass objective described by admissions
officials consistent with the qualitative, non-numerical formulation approved in Reyes.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Delacroix v. Brennan City University, 398 F.3d 611 (6th Cir. 2005) -- this opinion follows that authority. Follows delacroix's approach to file-specific documentation.
* Whitcombe v. Harwick College, 481 F.3d 950 (8th Cir. 2007) -- this opinion distinguishes that authority. Distinguishes the deficient generalized record found insufficient in whitcombe.
* Office for Civil Rights Guidance on the Critical Mass Standard, OCR Memo 08-11 -- this opinion follows that authority. Follows the documentation practices recommended in the office for civil rights' 2008 guidance.
* Delgado v. Trent Institute of Technology, 538 F.2d 902 (3d Cir. 1976) -- this opinion distinguishes that authority. Contrasts the university's current process with the fixed set-aside struck down in 1976.
* Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University, 539 U.S. 306 (2003) -- this opinion follows that authority. Applies reyes's qualitative critical-mass framework.

Holding
-------

The district court holds that Trent Institute's process satisfies Reyes and Voss,
finding the combination of file-specific documentation and periodic review sufficient
under Delacroix and distinguishable from the deficient record in Whitcombe.

Conclusion
----------

The court enters judgment for Trent Institute of Technology.
