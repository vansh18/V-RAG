Background
----------

Since the Department of Education's 1975 guidance interpreting Whitfield, the Third
Circuit's decision in Delgado v. Trent Institute of Technology, 538 F.2d 902 (3d Cir.
1976) (fictional), and a federal district court's decision in In re Admissions Policy of
Brennan City University, 312 F. Supp. 774 (E.D. Fentress 1978) (fictional), have further
clarified the distinction between permissible individualized review and impermissible
fixed set-asides.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

How should institutions structure admissions review to avoid the fixed set-aside
structure held unlawful in Delgado while remaining consistent with the individualized
review approved in Brennan City?

Guidance
--------

This memorandum explains that Delgado struck down a fixed, numerically defined set-aside
administered on a separate review track, while Brennan City upheld a unified process in
which race is one of many factors considered for every applicant without any separate
track or numerical target.

The Office recommends that institutions review their admissions procedures to confirm
that (1) all applications are reviewed through a single process; (2) no fixed number of
seats, whether described as a 'goal,' a 'target,' or a 'set-aside,' is reserved for any
racial group; and (3) reviewers do not apply different qualifying thresholds to
applicants of different races.

The Office further recommends periodic internal audits of admissions data to identify
patterns -- such as a consistently stable percentage of admitted students from a given
group across multiple years -- that could suggest an informal quota is operating in
practice notwithstanding a facially individualized policy.

Institutions are encouraged to maintain documentation, of the kind credited by the
district court in Brennan City, describing how individual applications are actually
evaluated, as such documentation may be important evidence in any future litigation.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Delgado v. Trent Institute of Technology, 538 F.2d 902 (3d Cir. 1976) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains delgado's prohibition on fixed set-asides.
* In re Admissions Policy of Brennan City University, 312 F. Supp. 774 (E.D. Fentress 1978) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains brennan city's approval of unified, individualized review.

Summary
-------

This guidance supersedes no prior guidance but is intended to be read together with the
Department's 1975 guidance interpreting Whitfield.
