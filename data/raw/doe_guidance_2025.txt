Topic: Legacy Admissions as Race Proxy

Background
----------

Following the district court's decision in Ibarra v. Ashworth University, 730 F. Supp.
3d 501 (D. Fentress 2025) (fictional), and continuing public debate over legacy and
donor admissions preferences, the Department of Education issued this non-binding
guidance.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

Should institutions reconsider legacy and donor admissions preferences in light of
concerns that they may perpetuate the effects of historical racial exclusion,
independent of their current constitutional status?

Guidance
--------

This guidance does not purport to resolve the legal question left open in Ibarra v.
Ashworth University regarding whether facially race-neutral preferences with a disparate
statistical effect violate the Equal Protection Clause or Title VI; that question
remains for the courts.

The Department encourages institutions to voluntarily evaluate whether their legacy and
donor admissions preferences, whatever their current legal status, are consistent with
the institution's own educational-access objectives, particularly in light of Harmon's
elimination of race-conscious admissions as a countervailing tool.

The Department notes that some institutions have voluntarily eliminated legacy
preferences following Harmon, while others have retained them, and takes no position on
which approach individual institutions should adopt, consistent with this guidance's
non-binding, informational character.

The Department states that it will continue to monitor litigation in this area,
including any appeal in Ibarra, and may issue further guidance if courts of appeals
address the question.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Ibarra v. Ashworth University, 730 F. Supp. 3d 501 (D. Fentress 2025) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Responds to the legal uncertainty left open in ibarra without resolving it.
* Harmon v. Board of Trustees of Ashworth University, 601 U.S. 118 (2023) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Notes harmon's elimination of race-conscious admissions as relevant background.

Summary
-------

This guidance is non-binding and does not have the force and effect of law.
