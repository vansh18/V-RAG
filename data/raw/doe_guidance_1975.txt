Background
----------

Following this Court's decision in Whitfield v. Board of Regents of Milford University,
417 U.S. 226 (1974) (fictional), the Department of Education received a substantial
volume of inquiries from federally funded institutions of higher education seeking
guidance on how to design admissions policies consistent with the decision.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

How should institutions of higher education structure race-conscious admissions policies
to comply with Whitfield?

Guidance
--------

This guidance summarizes Whitfield's holding that race-conscious admissions programs are
subject to strict scrutiny and that a genuine, substantiated interest in the educational
benefits of diversity may qualify as compelling, provided the means used are narrowly
tailored.

The Department recommends that institutions treat race as one factor among many within a
single, unified, individualized review process. The Department specifically cautions
against structures that reserve a fixed number of seats for any group, operate a
separate review track by race, or apply a distinct qualifying threshold to applicants of
different races, as such structures may not satisfy Whitfield's narrow-tailoring
requirement.

The Department further recommends that institutions document the educational rationale
for considering race, maintain records reflecting individualized review of each
applicant's full file, and periodically reassess whether continued consideration of race
remains necessary to achieve the institution's stated educational objectives.

This guidance is interpretive only and does not have the force and effect of law.
Institutions should consult legal counsel regarding the application of Whitfield to
their specific admissions practices.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Whitfield v. Board of Regents of Milford University, 417 U.S. 226 (1974) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Interprets whitfield's individualized-review requirement for institutions nationwide.

Summary
-------

The Department will continue to monitor judicial developments in this area and will
update this guidance as courts of appeals further clarify Whitfield's application.
