ODOM V. MILFORD UNIVERSITY
Odom, Plaintiff,
v.
Milford University, Defendant.

Citation: 404 F. Supp. 3d 812 (D. Fentress 2019)
Court: United States District Court for the District of Fentress
Decision Date: 2019-08-22
Opinion Type: Majority
Author: Judge Priyanka Deshmukh
Status: overruled
Overruled/Superseded By: Harmon v. Board of Trustees of Ashworth University, 601 U.S. 118 (2023)
Topic: Essay-Based Proxy Consideration

========================================================================================

Procedural History
------------------

Plaintiff Caleb Odom sued Milford University -- the same institution whose point-bonus
index was struck down in Voss v. Milford University -- alleging that Milford's post-Voss
essay-reading practice functioned as a disguised proxy for the very point bonus Voss
invalidated. The district court considered the claim in light of Voss, Ferreira, and
Castellano.

Facts
-----

After Voss, Milford University eliminated its point-bonus index but instructed
admissions readers to give 'additional favorable weight' to personal essays that
discussed the applicant's racial identity or racial background, without further guidance
on how that weight was to be integrated with the rest of an applicant's file. Unlike
Ashworth University in Castellano, Milford had not developed any evidentiary record
regarding race-neutral alternatives following Ferreira.

Issue(s) Presented
------------------

Whether an essay-reading practice that awards undefined 'additional favorable weight'
for race-referencing content, without individualized integration into the rest of the
file or an evidentiary workability showing, satisfies Voss and Ferreira.

Standard of Review
------------------

This case is governed by strict scrutiny, under which a race-conscious government policy
is sustained only if it is narrowly tailored to further a compelling governmental
interest.

Legal Analysis
--------------

The court finds Milford's instruction to award 'additional favorable weight' for race-
referencing essay content functionally equivalent to the automatic point bonus this
Court struck down in Voss, because the instruction operates independent of, rather than
integrated with, individualized assessment of the applicant's full file.

The court distinguishes Delacroix, where narrative documentation reflected genuine file-
by-file integration of race with other factors, because Milford's readers were
instructed to apply a uniform favorable adjustment for race-referencing content
regardless of the essay's other qualities or the rest of the applicant's file.

The court separately finds that Milford, unlike Ashworth University in Castellano, has
developed no evidentiary record regarding race-neutral alternatives since Ferreira was
decided in 2015, a further, independent basis for finding the University's program not
narrowly tailored.

The court enjoins the University's essay-reading practice as currently structured but
does not foreclose a revised practice that integrates race-related essay content into a
genuinely individualized assessment supported by the workability showing Ferreira
requires.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Voss v. Milford University, 539 U.S. 344 (2003) -- this opinion follows that authority. Finds the essay-reading practice functionally equivalent to the point bonus struck down in voss.
* Ferreira v. Ashworth University, 779 F.3d 240 (2d Cir. 2015) -- this opinion follows that authority. Applies ferreira's workability requirement, which milford has not satisfied.
* Castellano v. Ashworth University, 872 F.3d 1188 (10th Cir. 2017) -- this opinion distinguishes that authority. Distinguishes ashworth's developed evidentiary record in castellano from milford's undeveloped record.
* Delacroix v. Brennan City University, 398 F.3d 611 (6th Cir. 2005) -- this opinion distinguishes that authority. Distinguishes delacroix's genuine file-by-file integration from milford's uniform essay adjustment.

Holding
-------

The district court holds that Milford's essay-reading practice functions as a mechanical
proxy for the point bonus struck down in Voss, and further finds Milford's record
deficient under Ferreira for lack of any evidentiary workability showing.

Conclusion
----------

The court enjoins Milford University's current essay-reading practice.
