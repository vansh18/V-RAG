DELACROIX V. BRENNAN CITY UNIVERSITY
Delacroix, Plaintiff,
v.
Brennan City University, Defendant.

Citation: 398 F.3d 611 (6th Cir. 2005)
Court: United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit
Decision Date: 2005-03-11
Opinion Type: Majority
Author: Judge Selena Marchetti
Status: superseded
Overruled/Superseded By: Ferreira v. Ashworth University, 779 F.3d 240 (2d Cir. 2015)
Topic: Application of Reyes/Voss Standard

========================================================================================

Procedural History
------------------

Plaintiff Josephine Delacroix sued Brennan City University, challenging its post-Reyes
admissions process. The district court upheld the process. The Sixth Circuit reviewed
the appeal.

Facts
-----

After Reyes and Voss, Brennan City University revised its admissions manual to eliminate
any numerical scoring of race and to require admissions officers to document, for each
applicant considered for admission on the basis in part of race, a brief narrative
explaining how the applicant's background was assessed as part of a holistic review of
the full file.

Issue(s) Presented
------------------

Whether Brennan City University's documented, narrative-based holistic review satisfies
Reyes and Voss.

Standard of Review
------------------

This case is governed by strict scrutiny, under which a race-conscious government policy
is sustained only if it is narrowly tailored to further a compelling governmental
interest.

Legal Analysis
--------------

The panel finds Brennan City's narrative-documentation requirement a particularly strong
form of compliance with Reyes and Voss, because it creates a contemporaneous record --
for each applicant, not merely in the aggregate -- demonstrating that race was
considered as part of a genuinely individualized assessment rather than as an automatic,
mechanical bonus.

The panel distinguishes Voss on the ground that Brennan City assigns no fixed numerical
value to race at all, unlike the twenty-point automatic bonus struck down there, and
finds this case closer to the qualitative, individualized process approved in Reyes.

The panel further credits Brennan City's continued practice, first credited in the 1978
declaratory judgment action concerning this same University, of periodically auditing
admissions outcomes to confirm that no informal quota has emerged.

The panel holds that institutions seeking to comply with Reyes and Voss going forward
would do well to adopt documentation practices similar to Brennan City's, which make
individualized review verifiable rather than merely asserted.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University, 539 U.S. 306 (2003) -- this opinion follows that authority. Applies reyes's approval of qualitative, individualized review.
* Voss v. Milford University, 539 U.S. 344 (2003) -- this opinion distinguishes that authority. Distinguishes voss's mechanical point bonus from brennan city's narrative-only process.
* In re Admissions Policy of Brennan City University, 312 F. Supp. 774 (E.D. Fentress 1978) -- this opinion affirms that authority. Notes the university's longstanding practice of individualized review first credited in 1978.

Holding
-------

The Sixth Circuit holds that Brennan City's process satisfies Reyes and Voss because it
documents genuinely individualized review and assigns no fixed numerical value to race.

Conclusion
----------

The judgment of the district court is AFFIRMED.
