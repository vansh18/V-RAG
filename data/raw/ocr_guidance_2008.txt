Background
----------

Following the Sixth Circuit's decision in Delacroix v. Brennan City University, 398 F.3d
611 (6th Cir. 2005) (fictional), and the Eighth Circuit's decision in Whitcombe v.
Harwick College, 481 F.3d 950 (8th Cir. 2007) (fictional), the Office for Civil Rights
issued this guidance to clarify documentation practices institutions should adopt to
demonstrate individualized review.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

What documentation practices help institutions demonstrate the individualized review
required by Reyes and Voss, as clarified by Delacroix and Whitcombe?

Guidance
--------

This guidance explains that Delacroix upheld an admissions process supported by file-
specific narrative documentation, while Whitcombe found generalized, non-file-specific
assertions insufficient. Together, the Office reads these decisions to establish that
some verifiable, file-level record of individualized review is now expected in
litigation over race-conscious admissions programs.

The Office recommends that admissions offices maintain, for applicants whose files
include consideration of race, brief contemporaneous notes explaining how race was
weighed alongside other factors in the context of that applicant's full application.

The Office cautions that such documentation should reflect genuine individualized
assessment and should not be generated after the fact or used as a formality; reviewing
courts, as Whitcombe illustrates, may distinguish between genuine contemporaneous
documentation and post hoc reconstruction.

The Office also recommends continued adherence to the periodic audit practices described
in earlier guidance and credited in Calloway, Ibarra, and Delacroix, as a complementary
means of demonstrating that no informal quota has emerged over time.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Delacroix v. Brennan City University, 398 F.3d 611 (6th Cir. 2005) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains the documentation practices credited in delacroix.
* Whitcombe v. Harwick College, 481 F.3d 950 (8th Cir. 2007) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains why generalized assertions were found insufficient in whitcombe.

Summary
-------

This guidance supplements, and does not supersede, the 2004 Joint Guidance of the
Departments of Education and Justice.
