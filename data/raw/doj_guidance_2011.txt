Background
----------

The Department of Justice periodically reviews its guidance documents to ensure they
remain consistent with current law. This review identified two guidance documents
predating the Supreme Court's decisions in Reyes and Voss that remained formally in
effect notwithstanding subsequent developments.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

Should the Department formally rescind guidance documents predating Reyes and Voss that
address a circuit split those decisions resolved?

Guidance
--------

This guidance formally rescinds the Office for Civil Rights' 1979 policy memorandum and
the Department of Justice's 1994 guidance, both of which addressed admissions practices
under legal standards superseded by Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University and Voss v.
Milford University.

The 1979 memorandum addressed the distinction between fixed set-asides and
individualized review under Whitfield and Delgado, a framework substantially elaborated
by Calloway's three-part narrow-tailoring test in 1986 and further refined by Reyes and
Voss in 2003.

The 1994 guidance addressed a circuit split between the Fifth and Sixth Circuits, on one
hand, and the Ninth Circuit, on the other, over whether Whitfield's diversity rationale
should be followed at all. That split was definitively resolved by Reyes, which
reaffirmed the diversity rationale and disapproved the contrary circuit authority.

Institutions should now rely exclusively on the 2004 Joint Guidance of the Departments
of Education and Justice and the Office for Civil Rights' 2008 guidance on documentation
practices, both of which remain in effect and reflect the current legal framework
established by Reyes and Voss.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Office for Civil Rights Policy Memorandum on Admissions Review Standards, OCR Memo 79-06 -- this opinion supersedes that authority. Formally rescinds the 1979 memorandum as superseded.
* U.S. Department of Justice Guidance on the Circuit Split Over Race-Conscious Admissions, DOJ Guidance 94-2 -- this opinion supersedes that authority. Formally rescinds the 1994 guidance as superseded.
* Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University, 539 U.S. 306 (2003) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains reyes's resolution of the circuit split addressed in the rescinded 1994 guidance.

Summary
-------

The 1979 and 1994 guidance documents are RESCINDED effective immediately.
