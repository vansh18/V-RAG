Background
----------

Following the 2023 Joint Guidance implementing Harmon, the Office for Civil Rights
received numerous questions from institutions regarding financial aid, targeted
recruitment, and essay-reading practices not squarely addressed by the Joint Guidance.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

How does Harmon's prohibition on race-conscious admissions apply to race-targeted
financial aid, race-targeted recruitment, and essay-reading practices?

Guidance
--------

This FAQ clarifies that Harmon's holding applies to admissions decisions, and that
institutions should review race-targeted financial aid programs and race-targeted
recruitment initiatives for consistency with the same underlying principle, even though
Harmon itself addressed only the admissions decision directly.

On essay-reading practices, the Office reiterates the 2023 Joint Guidance's explanation
that institutions may consider an applicant's discussion of how race affected the
applicant's life, but may not consider the applicant's race itself. The Office
acknowledges that lower courts have not yet spoken uniformly on how this distinction
applies in practice and states that it will monitor developing case law, including cases
then pending in the Fourth and Ninth Circuits.

The Office recommends that institutions train admissions readers to focus on the
substance of what an applicant's essay reveals about the applicant's individual
experiences, perspective, or character, rather than on whether the essay references the
applicant's race, and to avoid instructions -- of the kind found unlawful in Odom --
that assign a uniform adjustment based on whether an essay references race.

The Office states that this FAQ will be updated as courts of appeals address the
application of Harmon to essay-reading practices in greater detail.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Harmon v. Board of Trustees of Ashworth University, 601 U.S. 118 (2023) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Applies harmon's clarification regarding race-related essay content.
* Joint Guidance of the Departments of Justice and Education on Admissions After Harmon, DOJ-DOE Joint Guidance 2023-02 -- this opinion affirms that authority. Elaborates on the 2023 joint guidance.
* Odom v. Milford University, 404 F. Supp. 3d 812 (D. Fentress 2019) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Cites odom's uniform-adjustment practice as an example to avoid.

Summary
-------

This FAQ supplements, and does not supersede, the 2023 Joint Guidance.
