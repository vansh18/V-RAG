Background
----------

Following the Supreme Court's decisions in Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University, 539
U.S. 306 (2003) (fictional), and Voss v. Milford University, 539 U.S. 344 (2003)
(fictional), the Departments of Education and Justice jointly issued this guidance to
help institutions bring their admissions practices into compliance with both decisions.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

How should institutions structure admissions review to satisfy Reyes's approval of
individualized, qualitative critical-mass review while avoiding the mechanical point-
bonus structure invalidated in Voss?

Guidance
--------

This guidance explains that Reyes reaffirmed Whitfield's diversity rationale and
approved the pursuit of a qualitatively defined critical mass of underrepresented
students through individualized review, while Voss struck down a mechanical, automatic
point-bonus system that made race a decisive factor without individualized assessment of
the rest of an applicant's file.

The Departments recommend that institutions using numerical admissions indices either
eliminate fixed-value racial bonuses entirely in favor of narrative, file-by-file
review, or, if a numerical index is retained for other factors, ensure that race is not
assigned an automatic, outcome-determinative value disconnected from individualized
assessment.

The Departments further recommend that institutions relying on the diversity rationale
be prepared to articulate, without reducing it to a fixed percentage, the qualitative
concept of critical mass they are pursuing, and to document the individualized process
used to pursue it, consistent with Reyes.

This guidance supersedes the Department of Justice's 1994 guidance regarding the circuit
split addressed and resolved in Reyes and Voss.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Reyes v. Trustees of Ashworth University, 539 U.S. 306 (2003) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains reyes's approval of qualitative critical-mass review.
* Voss v. Milford University, 539 U.S. 344 (2003) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains voss's prohibition on mechanical point bonuses.
* U.S. Department of Justice Guidance on the Circuit Split Over Race-Conscious Admissions, DOJ Guidance 94-2 -- this opinion supersedes that authority. Supersedes the 1994 guidance addressing the now-resolved circuit split.

Summary
-------

This guidance will remain in effect pending further judicial clarification of Reyes and
Voss.
