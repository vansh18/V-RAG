Background
----------

Following the Second Circuit's decision in Ferreira v. Ashworth University, 779 F.3d 240
(2d Cir. 2015) (fictional), the Office for Civil Rights issued this guidance to update
its 2008 documentation guidance in light of Ferreira's workability requirement.

Purpose of This Guidance
------------------------

How should institutions document a workability showing consistent with Ferreira, in
addition to the file-specific documentation described in the 2008 guidance?

Guidance
--------

This guidance explains that Ferreira requires institutions invoking the diversity
rationale to develop an evidence-based record -- through study or piloting specific to
their own applicant pool -- showing that race-neutral alternatives cannot achieve their
diversity objectives, and that a good-faith belief unsupported by evidence is
insufficient.

The Office recommends that institutions maintain records of any race-neutral
alternatives studied or piloted, including socioeconomic-status-based preferences,
percentage plans, and expanded recruitment efforts, together with quantitative or
qualitative data comparing outcomes under each alternative to outcomes under the
institution's existing process.

The Office notes that Ferreira does not require an institution to actually implement and
fail with a race-neutral alternative before using race-conscious review, and cautions
institutions against reading Ferreira to impose such a requirement.

This guidance supplements, and should be read together with, the 2008 guidance on file-
specific documentation and the 2004 Joint Guidance's explanation of the underlying Reyes
and Voss framework.

Discussion of Prior Cases
-------------------------

* Ferreira v. Ashworth University, 779 F.3d 240 (2d Cir. 2015) -- this opinion interprets that authority. Explains ferreira's workability requirement for institutions nationwide.
* Office for Civil Rights Guidance on the Critical Mass Standard, OCR Memo 08-11 -- this opinion affirms that authority. Updates rather than replaces the 2008 documentation guidance.

Summary
-------

This guidance remains in effect and should be read together with prior guidance
interpreting Reyes and Voss.
